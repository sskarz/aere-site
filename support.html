Support

Frequently asked

How do I turn on the keyboard?
  1. Open the Aere app and tap Open Settings (or go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard).
  2. Tap Keyboards and turn on Aere Keyboard.
  3. Turn on Allow Full Access if you want dictation and your settings (see below).
  4. In any app, touch and hold 🌐 (or the globe below the keyboard) and choose Aere Keyboard.

The app's Setup Guide walks through these steps and ticks them off as the keyboard shows up.

What is Full Access, and why does Aere need it?

iOS keeps keyboards from other developers in a sandbox. Full Access lifts some limits, and iOS warns that a keyboard with Full Access could send what you type over the network. Aere's keyboard has no network code and sends nothing anywhere. It uses Full Access only for:

  • the microphone, for dictation;
  • the shared storage between the app and the keyboard, so your settings, learned words and My Info reach the keyboard;
  • the clipboard chip, which reads the clipboard only when you tap it.

Without it you can still type, swipe, autocorrect and use emoji. Details are in the privacy policy.

Dictation isn't working
  • Full Access must be on: Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Aere Keyboard.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition must be allowed. Open Aere › Dictation and tap Allow, or turn them on in Settings › Aere.
  • Your iPhone's language needs on-device speech recognition. On iOS 26, the Dictation page shows whether Apple's speech model is installed or still downloading; until it's ready, Aere uses the classic on-device recognizer.
  • The mic is hidden in password, code and card fields on purpose (privacy mode).
  • Another app using the microphone, such as a call or a recording, can block dictation. Finish it and try again.
  • Dictation stops after about 8 seconds of silence. If it was cut off, tap Paste Dictation in the suggestion bar to get your words back (it's kept for 10 minutes).

Still stuck? Restart the app you're typing in, switch to another keyboard and back, or restart your iPhone.

Why does it switch to Apple's keyboard for passwords and codes?

iOS only shows AutoFill (saved passwords, passkeys, Hide My Email and codes from Messages) on Apple's own keyboard. Keyboards from other developers can't show it.

So in sign-in and code fields Aere shows a Passwords or AutoFill Code chip. Tap it to switch to the next keyboard, fill in your details with AutoFill, then tap 🌐 to come back. In some secure fields iOS switches to its own keyboard automatically. You can hide the chip in Aere › Layout.

In password, code and card fields Aere also turns on privacy mode: no learning, predictions, swipe or dictation there.

Dictation cleanup doesn't do anything

Cleanup uses Apple Intelligence, which needs iOS 26 or later on a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (and its model downloaded). Aere › Dictation Cleanup shows the current status. When it isn't available, your words are inserted as you said them.

How do I reset Aere?
  • Learned words: Aere › Typing › Reset Keyboard Dictionary.
  • Setup: Aere › Setup Guide runs the setup steps again.
  • Everything: delete the Aere app. That removes the keyboard and everything it stored on your iPhone. Reinstall from the App Store to start fresh.
Do I need a foldable iPhone?

No. Aere works on any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. On iPhone Duo it adapts when you fold and unfold, and can split the keyboard for your thumbs.

Does Aere collect anything?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no servers. Everything stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
