Aere

A keyboard built for iPhone Duo, folded and unfolded. Type, swipe and dictate on any iPhone, and nothing you say or type ever leaves it.

What's inside

Feels like the iPhone keyboard. Does a little more.

Aere matches the keys, sizes and gestures you already know, then adds dictation in the suggestion bar and a layout that changes shape with your phone.

Folded & unfolded

One keyboard, two shapes

Folded, Aere fills the narrow screen like the iPhone keyboard does. Unfolded, it can split for thumb typing, with a 1–0 number row a tap away in the ••• menu. On a regular iPhone it's simply a familiar keyboard.

On-device dictation

Dictate without switching apps

Tap the mic in the suggestion bar, talk, and tap ✓. Or hold the mic and let go to insert. Say "comma" or "new line" for punctuation. Recognition runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device speech model, and if dictation is cut off you get a Paste Dictation chip to recover it.

AI cleanup

Tidy text, your words

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Aere can clean up a dictation before inserting it: drop the "um"s, fix punctuation and capitals, in the style you pick. It runs on device, and Undo Cleanup gives back exactly what you said.

Autocorrect + swipe

Fast, forgiving typing

Autocorrect and predictions that learn the words you keep, slide-to-type, a space-bar trackpad, long-press accents, and quick numbers from the 123 key.

Emoji

Every emoji, searchable

A full emoji keyboard with search, recents and skin tones, one tap from the space bar.

Smart chips

The right shortcut, in context

The suggestion bar offers what fits the moment: paste a copied number or link, fill in your own email, phone or address from My Info, recover an interrupted dictation, or undo a cleanup.

Privacy mode

Quiet in password fields

In password, one-time-code and card fields Aere stops learning, predicting and logging. A Passwords chip hands you to Apple's keyboard so iOS AutoFill can fill them in.

Feels native

Made to match

Key sizes, sounds, haptics and the globe and dictation keys follow the iPhone keyboard, measured key by key, so your thumbs don't have to relearn anything.

Private by design

Your keyboard sees everything. Ours keeps it.

Aere has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Everything it does, it does on your iPhone.
